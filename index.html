Privacy Policy — Saver

Last updated: 2026-10-28 · Applies to the Chrome extension Saver — Media Archive for Instagram

Short version: Saver runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It does not require an account. Media files go directly from the platform's servers to your own computer; they never pass through a server operated by us.

1. What this extension does

Saver is a local-first media archiver for Instagram. It lets you save photos, videos, Reels and Stories from pages you can already access in your browser to your own computer. There is no sign-in, no cloud account, and no third-party server.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Specifically:

  • No account information, passwords, or cookies are collected.
  • No browsing history is collected.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers are used.
  • No media files, page URLs, captions, or account handles are uploaded to us.

3. Data stored on your device

The extension uses Chrome's chrome.storage.local API to store:

  • your settings (file naming template, filters, theme, panel position);
  • a local index of files you have saved, so the extension can avoid duplicates and resume an interrupted run;
  • license status, if you activate a Pro license.

This data stays on your computer. It is not synced to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the extension removes this local storage, subject to Chrome's own storage cleanup behavior.

4. Network access

The extension requests access to:

  • https://www.instagram.com/* — to read the page you are already viewing;
  • https://*.cdninstagram.com/* and https://*.fbcdn.net/* — to read the media files referenced by that page.

These requests go directly from your browser to Instagram's servers. They do not pass through any server operated by us. The extension does not use remote code; all code is packaged with the extension.

5. Downloads

When you choose to save media, the extension uses Chrome's downloads permission to write the file to your Downloads folder. You choose what to save. The extension does not download media in the background without a user action.

6. What the extension does not do

  • It does not log in to Instagram, import cookies, or bypass paywalls or private accounts.
  • It does not like, follow, unfollow, post, message, or automate account actions.
  • It does not repost, redistribute, or monetize content.
  • It does not sell or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

7. Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can clear the extension's local data at any time from the extension's settings page, or by removing the extension from Chrome. Because we do not hold any data about you, there is no server-side record to delete.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's release notes.
